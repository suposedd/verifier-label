# Verifier Label

Ever stumbled upon a level, and wondered -
"Who could've verified this?"

This mod shows who verified the level, right under the creator's name.

## Features

- **Video button** that opens the verification video (or a showcase depending on what AREDL puts there). (TOGGLEABLE IN MOD'S SETTINGS)
- **2P levels**: if a level is two-player, you can switch between the solo and two-player verification 
(NOT ALL 2P AREDL ENTRIES HAVE TWO VERIFIERS, so don't worry if there's only 1 verifier displayed).
- **Legacy status**: if a level was ever on the AREDL, but got downgraded to an Insane Demon, the label will appear White instead of Gold. (TOGGLEABLE IN MOD'S SETTINGS)
